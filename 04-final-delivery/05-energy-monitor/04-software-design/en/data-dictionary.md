## 2. Model rules

- **One shared schema, one owner per table.** Each table is created, migrated and written by a single service. The others get that data through the internal API (`/internal/**` with the `X-Internal-Token` header) or through RabbitMQ events.
- **Foreign keys only inside a service.** References to tables of another service (`user_id`, `home_id`, `device_id`, `measurement_id`, `consumption_level_id`) are `VARCHAR(10)` columns without a `FOREIGN KEY`, validated by the application; the *Constraint* column shows them as "application reference, no FK".
- **Identifiers** `VARCHAR(10)` generated by the application (for example `user000001`).
- **Audit and soft delete:** business tables have `created_at`, `updated_at` and `deleted_at`; a row with `deleted_at` counts as deleted.
- **Transactional outbox:** every service that publishes events has its `*_outbox` table (security, device, measurement, home, alert and recommendation). The notification service only consumes events and has no outbox.
- **Dates:** `TIMESTAMP` in UTC; the newer tables (outboxes and `pending_registration`) use `DATETIME(6)` with microseconds.

## 5. Seed data

| **Table** | **Changeset** | **Content** |
|---|---|---|
| password_policy | security-019 | Default policy `pol0000001`: 8 to 64 characters, upper case, digits and symbols required, 90-day expiry (only when the table is empty). |
| system_role, permission, system_role_permission | security-025 | Global role `ADMIN` (`rol0000001`) with the permissions `USER.READ` (`prm0000001`), `USER.MANAGE` (`prm0000002`) and `ROLE.MANAGE` (`prm0000003`). No account gets the role: the first administrator is granted with `make-admin.sh` of the energy-monitor-infra repository. |
| appliance_type | device-006 | refrigerator, air_conditioner, lighting, washing_machine, television, microwave, computer, water_heater and other (`appl000001` to `appl000009`). |
| consumption_level | measurement-003 | LOW [0, 500) W, MEDIUM [500, 1500) W, HIGH [1500, 3000) W and CRITICAL [3000, 1,000,000) W of active power. |
| home_type | home-005 | house, apartment, studio, country_house, cabin and other. |

The default limits of a home (6 kWh per day and 180 kWh per month) are not seed data: they are configuration of the home service (`daily-limit` and `monthly-limit` in its `application.yaml`).
